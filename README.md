# Me Before You (2016)

![Me Before You]

## 🎬 Movie Information
- **Genre:** Romance, Drama
- **Director:** Thea Sharrock
- **Actors:** Emilia Clarke, Sam Claflin
- **Based on:** A novel by Jojo Moyes

## 📖 Summary
*Me Before You* is about Louisa Clark, who becomes a caregiver for Will Traynor, a paralyzed man. As they spend time together, they develop a special connection.

## ❤️ Lessons
1. Appreciate life.
2. Love can change people.
3. Be brave and try new things.

## ⭐ My Rating
**9/10** – A beautiful and heartbreaking movie worth watching.
